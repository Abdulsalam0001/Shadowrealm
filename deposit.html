How it works

Secure payment flow

  1. Sign in or create your Shadowrealm account.
  2. Generate a membership invoice and copy the exact BTC amount and address.
  3. Send Bitcoin on the Bitcoin mainnet only. Check the address carefully before confirming.
  4. Submit the transaction ID. Shadowrealm checks live blockchain data and required confirmations.
  5. When payment is verified, your dashboard updates and full catalogue access is enabled.
Never share your wallet seed phrase, private keys, passwords or one-time authentication codes. A submitted transaction ID is not proof of payment by itself.
